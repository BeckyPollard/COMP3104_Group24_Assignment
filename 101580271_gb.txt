George Brown Polytechnic

George Brown Polytechnic, formerly known as George Brown College, is a
public college of applied arts and technology located in downtown
Toronto, Ontario. It was founded in 1967 and is named after George
Brown, the founder of The Toronto Globe newspaper and one of the Fathers
of Confederation. In October 2025, the college announced it was
rebranding as George Brown Polytechnic to better reflect the range of
programs it offers, including diplomas, certificates, bachelor's degrees,
and its first master's program in construction management. The school is
known for hands-on, career-focused learning and close connections to
industry.

Campuses

George Brown Polytechnic has three main campuses in Toronto: St. James
(business, hospitality, and arts), Casa Loma (technology and
construction), and Waterfront (health sciences).
