You're a developer. You're an operations specialist. You work in DevOps.

There are a lot of tools at one's disposal nowadays for optimizing the process of development. A developer can utilize these tools to also take the responsibility of operations under their control. The CI/CD pipeline is one such area of automation that is under the umbrella of DevOps.

The DevOps lifecycle is designed to optimize the rapid delivery of high-quality software. Not only does it cover work in the planning, coding, building, testing, and release phases of development- it also contributes tot the overall culture of a team or project. At the project management level, DevOps requires continuous communication and shared responsibility among all software delivery stakeholders to innovate quickly and focus on quality from the start. At the technical level, DevOps requires a commitment to automated tools that keep projects moving within and between workflows. To adopt a DevOps culture, organizations must often break down silos and reorganize personnel into cross-functional, autonomous DevOps teams.

Better collaboration, accelerated delivery, greater reliability, quicker scaling, enhanced security... all this and more can be yours when you embrace the truth of the DevOps way.
