George Brown College is a college in Toronto, Ontario.

It renamed itself to George Brown Polytechnic in 2025 but I'm not going to call it that.

It has 3 main campuses in Toronto. I like the downtown St. James campus campus best, the worst one is probably Waterfront just because that part of town is a hassle to transit to easily.

I actually moved to Toronto in 2015 with ambitions to take a course at GBC, but it didn't work out. I'm still in Toronto though, 11 years later.
